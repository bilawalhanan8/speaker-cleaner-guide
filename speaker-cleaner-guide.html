Speaker Cleaner — Fix a Wet Phone Speaker Free

Speaker Cleaner is a free online tool that plays specially tuned sound waves to eject water and dust from your phone speaker and fix muffled or quiet audio — right in your browser, no app to install.

How to eject water from your speaker

  1. Open the Speaker Cleaner tool in your browser.
  2. Turn your volume up and hold the phone with the speaker facing down.
  3. Press play and let the sound waves push the water out of the speaker grill.

Clearing dust

Run the cleaning sound for 30–60 seconds to shake loose dust trapped in the speaker mesh. Repeat if the audio still sounds muffled.

Fixing muffled audio

If your speaker sounds quiet or distorted after getting wet, run the water-ejection sound two or three times, then test with music or a call.
